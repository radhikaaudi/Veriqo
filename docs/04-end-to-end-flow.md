The biggest risk in your Veriqo demo is not whether Gemini can describe a car photo. It is whether the entire flow produces an evidence-backed report, handles missing information honestly, and gives the buyer a useful next action. Your hackathon demo needs to prove that end to end.
[LIKELY] The strongest demonstration is one used car with several inspection photos, a registration check, a transparent risk assessment, and a shareable report — followed by a second test where the photos are poor or documentation is unavailable.
1. Your complete Veriqo demo flow

Step 1 — Upload car photos
Exterior, close-up damage, tyres and interior. Allow multiple images.

Step 2 — Gemini visual inspection
Identify visible issues, reference the relevant photo, explain uncertainty and recommend checks.


Step 3 — Registration/document check
Use an authorised data provider if integrated. Otherwise, clearly mark the demo lookup as simulated.


Step 4 — Deterministic checks
Apply explicit rules to visual findings, source-backed fields and missing evidence. Do not invent a probability of fraud or failure.


Step 5 — Final buyer report
Show findings, supporting evidence, verified facts, unknowns and what to do before paying.
